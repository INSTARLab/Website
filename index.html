For federal research partners

Put frontier science behind the question that matters.

INSTAR combines advanced AI, quantum research, high-performance computing, and domain science to turn ambitious objectives into testable programs. From energy and space to health, agriculture, and fundamental physics, we define the question, engineer the method, measure uncertainty, and deliver evidence, models, prototypes, or data a sponsor can evaluate.

For researchers

Join the INSTAR Fellowship

The INSTAR Fellowship is an open citizen-scientist program for people who want to work on difficult questions across AI, quantum science, and the broader sciences. Fellows receive structured mentorship, interdisciplinary scope, and room to pursue a serious research problem.
