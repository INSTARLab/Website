About Us

INSTAR Lab Inc. is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit research organization headquartered in Marietta, Ohio. We're a 501(c)(3) public charity (classified by the IRS under §170(b)(1)(A)(vi)), and as a working research institute we conduct our own scientific research and translate it into real-world public benefit. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law under IRC §170.

Our Mission

"The said corporation has a main purpose of advancing the Omniverse in scientific discovery to enhance research, development, theory, education, innovation, societal growth, sustainability, and stewardship of intelligent life forces by addressing challenges through transformative solutions and multidimensional exploration."

— INSTAR Lab Inc., Articles of Incorporation, Article 11

Our research spans AI & machine learning, quantum science and computing, data analytics, health and medicine, energy, high-performance computing, space and orbital systems, materials science, and the broad sciences. We work through the INSTAR Consortium — seven specialized partners, each contributing proven methodologies: Focus Hive (AI-driven workforce performance & development), World Enterprise Group (business accelerator & incubator), Dream Limited (capture management & procurement solutions), Curiosity Research Corp (SPARK pedagogy and Curiosity Coaching), Ravonics (IT federal contractor), Global Enterprise (international business & logistics), and Tao Learning (education in STEAM & P-20+).

What We Do

INSTAR Lab conducts applied research across the sciences and technology disciplines, trains researchers at every level through the INSTAR Fellowship — an open citizen-scientist program with no minimum degree requirement — and transfers knowledge to enterprise and government partners through STTR programs and collaborative engagements.

SUPPORTING PUBLIC-BENEFIT SCIENCE

As a 501(c)(3) public charity, INSTAR Lab depends on the generosity of donors and partners who believe that independent, rigorous science creates lasting public benefit. Contributions fund research programs, Consortium Fellow stipends, and technology transfer that no single institution could accomplish alone. Donations are tax-deductible under IRC §170 .
