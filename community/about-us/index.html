About Us

INSTAR Lab Inc. is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit research institute headquartered in Marietta, Ohio — Ohio's oldest town. We advance scientific knowledge across disciplines, from AI and quantum computing to health, energy, and materials, by bringing together leading researchers inside our Research Residences: converted Victorian homes with fiber-optic infrastructure and the institutional conditions in which important science thrives. Gifts to INSTAR are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Our Organization

Our Mission

INSTAR Lab is an independent research institute advancing scientific discovery to enhance research, development, theory, education, innovation, and societal growth — translating those advances into real-world benefit through the INSTAR Consortium and direct technology transfer. As an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) private operating foundation (NTEE U05: Science & Technology Research Institutes), we are accountable to the public interest, not to shareholders.

Our researchers work from INSTAR Research Residences — converted Victorian homes in Marietta, Ohio equipped with fiber-optic connectivity, scientific libraries, and quiet focus corners where rigorous thinking flourishes. We draw on the talents of Appalachian scientists and collaborate with university researchers and regional partner companies advancing education, applied AI, and industry-leading research. Every researcher who joins INSTAR gains genuine intellectual freedom, a broad interdisciplinary network, and institutional support unconstrained by departmental politics or commercially-driven agendas. Our north star: advancing civilization toward the Kardashev scale.

Our Organization

Our Team

INSTAR's research community spans principal investigators, research scientists, postdoctoral fellows, graduate students, and technical specialists drawn from diverse institutional backgrounds. Our administrative staff — encompassing grants management, legal counsel, facilities operations, and communications — creates the operational foundation that allows researchers to focus on their science.

Our leadership team and Board of Directors are now publicly introduced — including AI Lead Scientist and Board Member Suzanne Conejos, Board Member and AI Researcher Sean Hackney, Applied Scientist · Materials Physics Dr. Spoogmay Khan, and Research Scientist Maha Khan.

Our Organization

Our History

INSTAR Lab Inc. was incorporated and received IRS recognition as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in 2020, establishing its base in Marietta, Ohio — Washington County, in the heart of Appalachian Ohio. From that founding, INSTAR has built programs spanning the natural sciences, social sciences, and technology, and has assembled the INSTAR Consortium: a network of five specialized partners whose methods in R&D prioritization, technology-readiness assessment, creative problem-solving, finance strategy, and pedagogy accelerate research translation. Our growth is measured not in headcount but in the quality of questions we pursue and the rigor we bring to answering them.
