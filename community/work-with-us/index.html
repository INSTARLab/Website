WORK WITH US

INSTAR Lab Inc. is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit research institute — which means every collaboration we enter is structured around scientific merit and public benefit, not proprietary capture. We offer multiple engagement pathways for universities, national laboratories, enterprises, NGOs, and government agencies. Whether you are seeking a co-investigator, a sponsored research partner, a technology transfer channel, or a subject-matter advisor, INSTAR brings rigorous research capacity and a proven Consortium methodology to the table.

For Researchers

Join INSTAR through the INSTAR Fellowship — our open citizen-scientist program — visiting scientist appointments, or open research positions. We welcome researchers at all levels who bring intellectual curiosity and a genuine commitment to crossing disciplinary lines. No minimum degree is required for the Fellowship; selection is based on fit with our research culture. Apply at /fellowship/.

For Institutions

Universities, national laboratories, and research institutes partner with INSTAR through the INSTAR Consortium, bilateral research agreements, and shared facility access programs. These arrangements leverage complementary strengths to tackle problems that neither organization could address alone. INSTAR's nonprofit status ensures that intellectual outputs are managed for broad public benefit, making us a reliable co-investigator for grant-funded collaborative research.

For Industry

Companies engage with INSTAR through sponsored research agreements, STTR partnerships, technology licensing, and joint R&D programs. The INSTAR Consortium — comprising Focus Hive (AI-driven workforce performance & development), World Enterprise Group (business accelerator & incubator), Dream Limited (capture management & procurement solutions), Curiosity Research Corp (SPARK pedagogy and Curiosity Coaching), Ravonics (IT federal contractor), Global Enterprise (international business & logistics), and Tao Learning (education in STEAM & P-20+) — provides a structured, multi-disciplinary methodology that de-risks collaboration and accelerates time to application.

For Government

Federal and state agencies seeking an accountable, mission-driven nonprofit research partner will find in INSTAR a capable and transparent collaborator. As an Ohio-based 501(c)(3) with research programs spanning AI, quantum science, health, energy, HPC, and materials, INSTAR is positioned to support national-priority research initiatives. We welcome discussions with program officers and agency partners about technical advisory, sponsored research, and cooperative agreement structures.
