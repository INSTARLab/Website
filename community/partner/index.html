PARTNER WITH US

INSTAR Lab Inc. is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit research organization , headquartered in Marietta, Ohio. Contributions to INSTAR are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law under IRC §170. When you support INSTAR — through philanthropy, sponsored research, or institutional partnership — you invest in independent, public-benefit science conducted with full accountability and transparent stewardship. We design partnership and giving structures that align with your goals, from one-time gifts and sponsored projects to long-term strategic research alliances.
