Enterprise R&D — INSTAR Lab Technology Transfer

INSTAR Lab is a 501(c)(3) research institute that matures applied research in AI, secure software, sensing, simulation, and learning science — then transitions it to partner companies who commercialize it. A repeatable research-to-product pipeline aligned with the intent of SBIR/STTR programs.
