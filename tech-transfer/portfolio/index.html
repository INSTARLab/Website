RESEARCH-TO-COMMERCIALIZATION PORTFOLIO

INSTAR Lab is a 501(c)(3) research institute whose applied R&D finds its highest expression through partner companies that commercialize it. INSTAR-affiliated researchers contribute the underlying science and engineering that powers products spanning autonomous software delivery, multi-domain sensing and simulation, frontier generative AI, decision intelligence, and learning science. The portfolio below represents that continuum — from active research programs to products in production — with several at SBIR/STTR readiness for federal partnership engagement.

DROIDOPS INC — Autonomous Software Engineering & Secure Delivery

DroidOps Inc is a software company whose products were developed with foundational R&D contributions from INSTAR-affiliated researchers, advancing the frontier of autonomous and zero-trust software engineering.

Launch Sequence — Secure Supply-Chain Automation

A DroidOps product underpinned by INSTAR supply-chain security research. Every build artifact is cryptographically signed, scanned, and attested before deployment — producing auditable, immutable, compliance-aligned delivery pipelines. Reshapes how organizations ship software securely at scale, and is already in production use.

STTR Programs

Hypercraft — Governed Agentic AI Layer

A DroidOps product drawing on INSTAR research into safe agentic AI deployment. Hypercraft is a governed automation layer that lets autonomous AI agents operate safely within enterprise guardrails — bringing frontier agentic AI into regulated software engineering workflows without sacrificing auditability or control. In production.

STTR Programs

Mission Control — Real-Time Operations Console

A DroidOps product informed by INSTAR research in distributed infrastructure observability. Mission Control gives teams live visibility and command over complex cloud and GPU environments — turning sprawling infrastructure into a single, actionable operations surface. In production.

STTR Programs

Autonomous Engineering Agents — End-to-End AI Development

A DroidOps product pioneered with INSTAR autonomous-systems research. Long-lived AI agents autonomously design, build, test, and ship software products end-to-end — redefining what a software engineering team can accomplish and opening a new era of autonomous software production. In production.

STTR Programs

VIPER DYNAMICS — Multi-Domain Sensing, Perception & Simulation

Viper Dynamics develops sensing, perception, and simulation products for defense and enterprise customers, built on foundational R&D from INSTAR-affiliated researchers in multi-domain fusion, computer vision, and biometric security.

Viper-Sim — AI-Native Simulation & Wargaming

A Viper Dynamics product grounded in INSTAR simulation and course-of-action research. Viper-Sim delivers AI-native, multi-domain simulation and wargaming for mission rehearsal — faster than real time, fully explainable, and interoperable with established defense standards. Purpose-built for the pace of modern operational decision-making. In production (defense).

STTR Programs

Viper-World — Live Multi-Domain Common Operational Picture

A Viper Dynamics product informed by INSTAR multi-domain data fusion research. Viper-World fuses live feeds — air, space, weather, hazards, maritime — into a single navigable 3D globe, giving operators a real-time, unified picture of a complex operational environment. In production.

STTR Programs

Viper-Touch — Frontier Multi-Modal Biometric Authentication

A Viper Dynamics product backed by INSTAR biometric security research. Viper-Touch extracts rich identity signal from commodity sensors with built-in anti-spoofing — advancing zero-trust security beyond passwords and traditional credentials to something that cannot be borrowed, shared, or stolen. Advanced R&D.

STTR Programs

Viper-Sight — AI-Powered Perception & Capture

A Viper Dynamics product drawing on INSTAR computer-vision research. Viper-Sight turns commodity cameras into mission-grade sensors — delivering AI-powered perception and scene analysis that extracts operationally relevant intelligence from unmodified optical hardware. In production.

STTR Programs

Agentic Hardware Research — Autonomous Sensor Capability Exploration

Frontier experimental research conducted by INSTAR-affiliated researchers on behalf of Viper Dynamics. Autonomous, hardware-in-the-loop research agents systematically explore and unlock latent capability in optical sensor hardware — a research modality that could not exist without the convergence of agentic AI and physical experimentation. Active research.

STTR Programs

DREAM LIMITED — Autonomous Capture & Decision Intelligence

Dream Limited develops AI-native products for opportunity intelligence and proposal automation, commercializing INSTAR research in autonomous document generation and opportunity discovery.

Dream Agent — Autonomous Proposal Intelligence

A Dream Limited product built on INSTAR research in autonomous document generation and organizational knowledge extraction. Dream Agent autonomously researches opportunities and produces high-quality proposals — reshaping how organizations pursue federal and commercial work by removing the bottleneck of human-hours-per-proposal. In production.

STTR Programs

Dream Track — Continuous Opportunity Discovery

A Dream Limited product informed by INSTAR research in semantic matching and federal data source integration. Dream Track continuously discovers and matches opportunities across federal funding and contract sources to an organization's capabilities — ensuring no relevant solicitation is missed and giving teams time to pursue work, not search for it. In production.

STTR Programs

HARDMAGIC — Generative AI & Inference Infrastructure

HardMagic develops generative AI products and inference infrastructure, with several offerings built directly on INSTAR research programs in large-model efficiency, multi-modal generation, and on-premises AI serving.

BLADE — Large-Model Compression Research

A HardMagic product grounded in INSTAR's proprietary large-model compression research. BLADE runs frontier-scale language models on commodity, single-GPU hardware while preserving task quality across code, legal, finance, and government domains — putting capabilities that normally require data-center clusters within reach of edge and cost-constrained deployments. TRL 6 / SBIR-STTR readiness.

STTR Programs

GPU Router — Production AI Inference Platform

A HardMagic product developed with INSTAR distributed systems research. GPU Router serves dozens of models across text, vision, audio, video, and 3D from an orchestrated on-premises GPU environment with elastic overflow to cloud capacity — demonstrating the viability of sovereign, on-premises AI infrastructure for organizations with data-residency or security requirements. In production.

STTR Programs

Inference Tuning — Systematic LLM Serving R&D

An ongoing INSTAR research program that feeds directly into HardMagic's product roadmap. A disciplined, evidence-driven program that systematically advances the efficiency and usable context of language-model serving on commodity hardware under rigorous quality controls — with every hypothesis and outcome captured as reproducible institutional knowledge. Active research.

STTR Programs

HardMagic Studio — Multimodal Generative AI Platform

A HardMagic product informed by INSTAR research in multimodal generation and human-AI interface design. HardMagic Studio turns natural-language intent into professional image, video, audio, and 3D content — making frontier generative AI accessible to non-experts and unlocking creative production at a scale previously reserved for specialized teams. In production.

STTR Programs

LoRA Lab — Efficient Fine-Tuning & Model Personalization

A HardMagic product backed by INSTAR research in parameter-efficient model adaptation. LoRA Lab lowers the cost and barrier of model customization — enabling consistent, personalized generative models without the compute overhead of full retraining, and opening high-quality customization to organizations that could not previously afford it. In production.

STTR Programs

AI SAFETY & TRUST — Adversarial Evaluation Research

Research conducted by INSTAR-affiliated researchers in collaboration with the Octo-Hurricane security group, focused on making agentic AI systems safer before they reach deployment.

Adversarial AI Evaluation — Red-Teaming Agentic Systems

INSTAR-affiliated researchers conduct red-teaming and adversarial stress-testing of agentic AI systems to surface safety failures before deployment — using open-source evaluation frameworks including Microsoft's PyRIT. This work informs responsible-AI guidelines for INSTAR's partner ecosystem and contributes to emerging evaluation methodology for AI systems in sensitive or regulated contexts. INSTAR is a research user and evaluator of these tools, not their author. Active research.

STTR Programs

RESEARCH GOVERNANCE — Hyper-Void / Hypermap

INSTAR's own research governance infrastructure, developed and operated by the Hyper-Void program, provides the institutional backbone for tracking SBIR/STTR pipelines and organizational knowledge across the INSTAR research enterprise.

Hypermap — Research Governance Knowledge Graph

Hypermap is a structured knowledge-graph platform that models organizations, programs, grants, and contracts as a navigable, version-controlled map. It is the system of record for INSTAR's active SBIR/STTR pipelines and institutional relationships — providing program managers and researchers with a single, authoritative view of the entire research enterprise. In production.

STTR Programs

LEARNING SCIENCE — Curiosity Research Corporation

Curiosity Research Corporation is a 501(c)(3) consortium member collaborating with INSTAR on applied learning-science research in human-capital development and measurable learner outcomes.

Dream Quest — Evidence-Based Learning Science Framework

An INSTAR and Curiosity Research Corporation consortium research initiative. Dream Quest is an evidence-based learning-science framework that measures learner growth through observable, real-world outcomes — moving beyond test scores to assess genuine human-capital development. Grounded in cognitive science and built for scalable deployment across diverse learning contexts. Active research.

RESEARCH FOCUS AREAS

INSTAR's research and technology capabilities span high-priority domains that align with federal R&D priorities and industry needs. These focus areas define where we are positioned to collaborate — whether as an STTR research partner, an enterprise R&D resource, or a sponsored-research host.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

INSTAR conducts research in applied machine learning, model compression, AI system design, and inference optimization across scientific discovery, government, and enterprise contexts. Our researchers work at the intersection of algorithm development and domain-specific deployment challenges.

Current Programs

HPC & Distributed Systems

INSTAR's HPC research addresses GPU cluster orchestration, distributed inference, Kubernetes-based workload scheduling, and reproducible benchmark harnesses. This work directly supports INSTAR's operational research infrastructure and informs scalable federal computing architectures.

Current Programs

Data Governance & Knowledge Systems

Research in this area addresses schema-validated knowledge graphs, entity resolution, version-controlled data models, and institutional knowledge management. Outputs support research governance, grant pipeline tracking, and organizational dependency mapping for complex research enterprises.

Current Programs

Quantum Computing

INSTAR's quantum computing research addresses algorithm design, error mitigation, and near-term application development. As quantum hardware matures, this work positions us — and our collaborators — to engage federal and industry programs at the leading edge of this transformative technology.

Current Programs

Space, Orbital & Geoscience

INSTAR brings research capability in space systems, orbital mechanics, remote sensing, and earth observation analytics. These competencies intersect with both commercial space sector needs and federal R&D priorities in national security, climate, and infrastructure monitoring.

Current Programs

Advanced Materials & Energy

Materials science and energy systems research represent core pillars of INSTAR's scientific mission. Our work in computational materials modeling, simulation-driven design, and energy storage and grid analytics supports translational applications in manufacturing, defense, and energy independence.

OUR PARTNERS

Open Data

Grounded in Open Data

The research programs in INSTAR's portfolio are grounded in transparent, publicly available datasets wherever the domain permits. Open data use improves reproducibility, enables external validation, and ensures that the partner companies who commercialize INSTAR's R&D inherit a verifiable scientific foundation. Below are the primary public data resources that inform our active programs.

  • Data.gov — U.S. federal open data portal; used for benchmarking, sector-specific datasets, and federal program alignment across multiple partner domains in this portfolio.
  • NIST — Standards, measurement science, and evaluation frameworks that underpin AI, cybersecurity, biometric security, and advanced materials research represented in this portfolio.
  • DOE OSTI — Department of Energy scientific and technical information; used for energy systems, HPC, and computational research programs.
  • NSF — Research funding landscape, award data, and scientific literature that inform INSTAR's research pipeline and SBIR/STTR program targeting.
