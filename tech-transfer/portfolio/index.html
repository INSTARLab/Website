APPLIED RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

The following projects represent original applied research conducted by INSTAR-affiliated researchers and collaborators, pursued in alignment with STTR research objectives. Several have reached TRL 6 or above and are positioned for federal partnership engagement. All work is carried out using INSTAR's computational infrastructure and governed by the Lab's research review process.

BLADE — Model Compression & Expert Pruning

BLADE (Budget Language Adaptable Distillation Enhancer) is a Mixture-of-Experts expert-pruning toolkit that compresses large language models to run on a single consumer GPU (24 GB) with CPU offload. It combines activation-norm expert scoring, shared-expert redundancy correction, non-uniform per-layer budgets selected via evolutionary search, and post-pruning router knowledge distillation. Domain-aware calibration covers code, legal, finance, and government tasks; outputs export to GGUF and are evaluated against standard benchmarks including HumanEval, MBPP, and MMLU. Demonstrated 25–40% expert removal with under approximately 1% quality regression. TRL 6 / SBIR-readiness.

STTR Programs

GPU Router — Distributed Multi-Model Inference

GPU Router is a production inference platform that routes requests across a local Kubernetes GPU cluster (9× RTX 3090, 50+ models spanning text, vision, audio, video, and 3D) with automatic LRU model load/eviction and overflow to cloud capacity when local resources are saturated. It provides dynamic GPU partitioning across modalities, an OpenAI-compatible API, schema-guided structured output, and per-user cost tracking and tiering — all backed by full IaC (Kustomize/ArgoCD) and end-to-end test coverage.

STTR Programs

Inference Tuning — Systematic LLM Serving R&D

This R&D program captures every serving-configuration hypothesis, measured outcome, and verdict — including negative results — as institutional knowledge. The program achieved approximately 96K usable context on a single RTX 3090 while enforcing a strict quality gate (≤1% relative regression across HumanEval, MBPP, long-context retrieval, structured tool calls, and throughput). A deterministic Kubernetes benchmark harness and living runbooks ensure reproducibility across experiments.

STTR Programs

Hypermap — Research Governance Knowledge Graph

Hypermap is a schema-validated, version-controlled knowledge graph that models organizations, products, grants/contracts, and their relationships as a navigable map — used to track active grant, SBIR, and STTR pipelines and organizational dependencies for a research enterprise. The project delivers a reusable specification and a reference implementation comprising a relational store, a REST API, and a force-directed graph UI.

STTR Programs

AI Safety & Red-Teaming Evaluation

INSTAR conducts AI safety and adversarial evaluation research using open-source red-teaming frameworks, including Microsoft's PyRIT (Python Risk Identification Toolkit for generative AI). This work informs the Lab's responsible-AI guidelines and supports the development of evaluation methodologies for AI systems deployed in sensitive contexts. INSTAR is an evaluator and research user of these tools, not their author.

STTR Programs

Partner on Applied AI Research

INSTAR is open to sponsored-research arrangements, STTR teaming partnerships, and enterprise R&D collaborations in AI/ML, HPC, and data infrastructure. We bring institutional nonprofit status, existing computational resources, and active research programs to partnership engagements. Contact us to discuss how INSTAR can support your R&D objectives.

RESEARCH FOCUS AREAS

INSTAR's research and technology capabilities span high-priority domains that align with federal R&D priorities and industry needs. These focus areas define where we are positioned to collaborate — whether as an STTR research partner, an enterprise R&D resource, or a sponsored-research host.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

INSTAR conducts research in applied machine learning, model compression, AI system design, and inference optimization across scientific discovery, government, and enterprise contexts. Our researchers work at the intersection of algorithm development and domain-specific deployment challenges.

Current Programs

HPC & Distributed Systems

INSTAR's HPC research addresses GPU cluster orchestration, distributed inference, Kubernetes-based workload scheduling, and reproducible benchmark harnesses. This work directly supports INSTAR's operational research infrastructure and informs scalable federal computing architectures.

Current Programs

Data Governance & Knowledge Systems

Research in this area addresses schema-validated knowledge graphs, entity resolution, version-controlled data models, and institutional knowledge management. Outputs support research governance, grant pipeline tracking, and organizational dependency mapping for complex research enterprises.

Current Programs

Quantum Computing

INSTAR's quantum computing research addresses algorithm design, error mitigation, and near-term application development. As quantum hardware matures, this work positions us — and our collaborators — to engage federal and industry programs at the leading edge of this transformative technology.

Current Programs

Space, Orbital & Geoscience

INSTAR brings research capability in space systems, orbital mechanics, remote sensing, and earth observation analytics. These competencies intersect with both commercial space sector needs and federal R&D priorities in national security, climate, and infrastructure monitoring.

Current Programs

Advanced Materials & Energy

Materials science and energy systems research represent core pillars of INSTAR's scientific mission. Our work in computational materials modeling, simulation-driven design, and energy storage and grid analytics supports translational applications in manufacturing, defense, and energy independence.
