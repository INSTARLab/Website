    Affiliation worksheet

    The register states the evidence, not the relationship.

    This register carries every partner organization and every named person the public site presents, next to what establishes them. Nothing here removes a name or a mark: the homepage and the consortium route are unchanged, and that is deliberate. What changes is that a reader can now see which affiliations the institution has confirmed and which it has only published.

    Evidence rule. An “unverified” row does not say the affiliation is false. It says no source in this repository, and no external record this register cites, establishes it.

    The claim

    The consortium, as the site states it.

    The INSTAR Consortium unites seven specialized partner organizations to advance applied research across AI, quantum science, health, energy, and the full sciences through shared methodology.

    The consortium route description in the research route ledger, and the partner-network section of the homepage, which renders the seven marks.

    This register can establish that the site names seven organizations and renders seven marks: that is repository-verified, and the rows below are exactly those seven. It cannot establish that seven is the number of organizations in the consortium.

    No source in this repository or in the external records this register cites states a consortium membership count, a consortium charter, or that the INSTAR Consortium is a legal entity, a joint venture, or a membership organization at all. The count is the site’s own, and the register records it as the site’s own.

    Publication boundary

    This register does not change the homepage.

    The partner names, marks and links on the consortium route and the homepage are public-facing branding and they are the owner’s call. This register flags the state of the evidence behind them; it does not remove, rename, relink or reorder any of it.

    Where an affiliation is not established, the honest output is a row that says so — not a source invented to make the row look clean, and not a deletion that hides the question. Closing any of the rows above needs a partner-provided record or an owner confirmation, and either one is a change to the data, not to this page.
