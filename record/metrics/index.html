    Method and limits

    What these counts cannot tell you

    Derived inventory

    Route, family, kind, indexability, and public media policy counts are stable build-time measures.

    Not connected

    No private analytics, Search Console, funding, award, project, or partnership system is included.

    Separate review

    Claims, citations, media rights, and external operating status require their own source and human review gates.
