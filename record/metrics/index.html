    Method and limits

    What these counts cannot tell you

    Derived inventory

    Route, family, kind, indexability, and public media policy counts are stable build-time measures. Inbound counts journey-step edges only — suggested next actions never inflate them.

    Automated capture signals

    Capture status, dimensions, and file sizes are automated browser observations of one frozen build, not human-verified quality. A current capture proves the page rendered — nothing about its claims, citations, or media rights.

    Separate review

    No private analytics, Search Console, funding, award, project, or partnership system is included. Claims, citations, media rights, and external operating status require their own source and human review gates.
