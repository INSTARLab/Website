    Resource use and measurement

    What a first-grant review, a donor, and a board member each ask for.

    A program officer opening a first grant asks how much of the money reaches program, who approved the figures, and how the work is measured. A donor asks where a gift goes and whether it is restricted. A board member asks whether a budget and a financial statement exist, and who signed them. This register carries those questions with the same basis-and-limit discipline as every other register here, and it publishes the ones nothing answers as absences rather than leaving them off the table.

    Read the row next to it. The program-service share is a filing figure carried by a state record, and the approval basis behind this room’s own measures is a management attestation. The register prints them in the same table because a reader who takes one without the other has an incomplete file.

    Publication boundary

    Keep the inference line visible.

    This register does not reproduce private personnel material, board minutes, resolutions, or the text of any policy. Those may exist outside the public route graph; their publication authority and privacy review are not established here.

    A name on a public page establishes that the page says so. It does not establish an appointment, a term, a fiduciary duty, or a qualification, and nothing in this register should be read as establishing one.
