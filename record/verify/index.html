External authoritative sources

Figures about the institution that the institution does not publish itself.

These are third-party records. The operational measures in this room publish only what an approver inside INSTAR Lab has signed, and everything else stays “Not reported” rather than being filled in from here. Where a filing does publish a figure, this register names the publisher, states the figures it carries, and keeps the limitation next to them.
