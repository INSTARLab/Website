Answered from published copy and the public record.

What is INSTAR Lab?

INSTAR Lab is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit research institute conducting applied research in artificial intelligence, quantum science, high-performance computing, health, energy, space, and the sciences for federal and institutional research partners.

  • About INSTAR Lab
  • Our mission
What is INSTAR Lab’s nonprofit standing?

INSTAR Lab Inc. is exempt under IRC §501(c)(3) and classified as a public charity under IRC §170(b)(1)(A)(vi), with a ruling effective 27 April 2020 and employer identification number 85-0845517.

The institution serves its IRS determination letter on this site, and reading that document confirms each of those fields. The public-charity classification is what makes contributions deductible.

  • Legal standing in the public record
  • IRS determination letter (PDF)
Is INSTAR Lab a private foundation?

No. INSTAR Lab is classified as a public charity under IRC §170(b)(1)(A)(vi), not a private foundation of any kind. An earlier version of this site misstated the classification, and the correction is recorded in the public record.

  • Recorded correction (COR-004)

How do I contact INSTAR Lab?

Email info@instarlab.org or call 929-229-2917. These are the contact details the site publishes.

One external source disagrees on the telephone number’s final digit: the Ohio Attorney General charitable-registration record shows (929) 222-2917. The record room carries that discrepancy as unresolved rather than guessing, so if a call fails, use email.

What fellowship pathways are available?

There are two R&D Scholar pathways. The International R&D Scholars Program is a voluntary, self-paced pathway with no fixed weekly commitment, for students, citizen scientists, independent researchers, engineers, and technologists building a credible research direction.

The Resident R&D Scholars Program is a structured and mentored pathway asking at least 20 hours per week, for early-career or transitioning researchers, engineers, scientists, and technologists preparing for demanding mission-focused research.

  • Compare the fellowship pathways
How do I apply for a fellowship?

INSTAR Lab provides the research orientation; US Fellows provides the R&D Scholar application and review path. Choose the pathway that matches your availability, read its official guide, then use the matching application link, which opens the shared US Fellows form with the program already identified.

  • Start at the fellowship overview
What should I leave out of an application or inquiry?

Never submit classified, export-controlled, proprietary, unpublished sensitive, clearance-identifying, or protected personal information through a public form.

  • Application preparation
How do I start a research or partnership conversation?

Bring a solicitation, contract requirement, research question, or partnership proposal. For federal research questions, include the program, solicitation, technical area, and desired outcome when available, plus the technical question, scope, timeline, data or facility constraints, and the decision the work must support.

  • Contact INSTAR Lab
