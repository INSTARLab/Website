Forms and our Salesforce CRM

This is not a cookie or an advertising tag. When you send a form, your submission and the campaign link you arrived from are stored in INSTAR Lab’s Salesforce CRM through a form service on Microsoft Azure. Click identifiers and first-visit data are stored only if the Advertising and campaign measurement category is on. The full list of what is stored, why, for how long and your rights is in the Privacy Policy. Read the Privacy Policy section.

Your privacy choices

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time, including switching every optional category off. The same control is the Privacy choices link in the footer of every page. A browser Global Privacy Control signal is honored: optional categories stay off until you choose.

Privacy requests

To ask what we hold about you, correct or delete it, withdraw consent, or opt out of sharing, emailinfo@instarlab.org with the subject “Privacy request”. Include your consent ID (shown in Privacy choices) if you want us to find your consent record. We answer within the time the law allows, and we never penalize anyone for making a request.
