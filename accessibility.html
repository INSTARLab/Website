Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Our Commitment

INSTAR Lab Inc. is committed to ensuring that its website is accessible to the widest possible audience, including individuals with disabilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and to meet the requirements of Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, as applicable to our programs and activities as a federally engaged nonprofit.

We recognize that achieving full accessibility is an ongoing process, not a single destination. We actively work to identify and remediate accessibility barriers on this Site and welcome feedback from users who encounter difficulties.

Measures We Take

To support accessible use of this Site, INSTAR Lab endeavors to:

  • Provide text alternatives (alt attributes) for meaningful images.
  • Use semantic HTML elements that support screen reader navigation.
  • Provide ARIA labels on interactive elements where native semantics are insufficient.
  • Ensure that form fields have associated visible labels.
  • Maintain sufficient color contrast for body text and interactive elements.
  • Support keyboard-only navigation for primary site functions.

Known Limitations

While we strive for broad accessibility, we acknowledge that some portions of this Site may not yet fully conform to WCAG 2.1 AA. We do not claim full certified compliance at this time. Areas we are actively working to improve include:

  • Keyboard accessibility of complex navigation mega-menus on desktop viewports.
  • Color contrast of certain decorative UI elements inherited from the underlying theme.
  • ARIA landmark roles on all page regions.

We are committed to addressing these issues as we continue to develop and maintain the Site.

Technical Specifications

Accessibility of this Site relies on the following technologies working together in your browser: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and WAI-ARIA. The Site is designed to be compatible with current versions of major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and assistive technologies including common screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver).

How to Report an Accessibility Issue

If you encounter a barrier that prevents you from accessing any content or feature on this Site, or if you have suggestions for how we can improve accessibility, please contact us:

  • Email: info@instarlab.org (include "Accessibility" in the subject line)
  • Phone: +1 855-221-4692
  • Mail: INSTAR Lab Inc., 125 Frederick St, Marietta, OH 45750-3407

We aim to respond to accessibility inquiries within five business days and to propose a remediation plan or alternative access method within ten business days where feasible.

Formal Complaints

If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may contact the U.S. Department of Justice, Civil Rights Division, Disability Rights Section, for information on filing a Section 508 or ADA Title III complaint.

Date of This Statement

This accessibility statement was prepared on June 15, 2026. It will be reviewed and updated periodically as the Site evolves.
