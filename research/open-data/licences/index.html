Open data catalogue

Open data licences in plain words.

The open data catalogue lists 60 public datasets from other publishers.17 of them are recorded under 6 standard licences that has an official deed or statute page, and this page summarises each of those. The other 43 are recorded under 34 wordings that is custom, mixed or unclear, so each is kept as the catalogue records it, with no summary here.

This is a summary, not legal advice; the licence text is what applies. The counts are made from the catalogue when the site is built (the catalogue was checked on 2026-10-23), and the summaries were read from the official pages listed under Sources on 2026-10-24. Licences can change, so check the publisher's page before you reuse a dataset.

CC BY 4.0 (7 datasets)

What it lets you do

  • Copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format, for any purpose, even commercially.
  • Remix, transform, and build upon the material, for any purpose, even commercially.

What it asks of you

  • Give appropriate credit, provide a link to the licence, and indicate if changes were made, in any reasonable manner but not in a way that suggests the licensor endorses you or your use.
  • Do not apply legal terms or technological measures that legally restrict others from doing anything the licence permits.

Worth knowing

  • You do not have to comply with the licence for elements of the material in the public domain, or where your use is permitted by an applicable exception or limitation.
  • No warranties are given, and the licence may not give you all the permissions you need for your use; rights such as publicity, privacy or moral rights may limit how you use the material.
