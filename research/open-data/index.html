Open Data & Public Datasets — INSTAR Lab

A curated, searchable reference list of third-party public datasets from federal portals, science agencies and repositories, organized by research area. INSTAR-published datasets: none yet.

Dataset catalogue

Public datasets by research area.

INSTAR-published datasets: none yet.

This is a curated reference list of 60 third-party public datasets from federal agencies, science portals and benchmark suites. INSTAR did not produce them, and a listing does not mean INSTAR uses a dataset in a specific project. Each entry links to the publisher's own page; licences and terms are summarised from that publisher's pages, were checked on 2026-10-23, and can change. Read the publisher's terms before reuse.

New to these licences? Read what each licence means in plain words.
