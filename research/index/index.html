Research index

A–Z index of research and partners.

One alphabetical list of the research areas, pages, briefs, datasets and partner affiliations published on this site. It is built from the site's own data and adds nothing to it. For the terms the program pages define, see the research glossary. For every published work the pages cite, see the research sources, and for how the program pages share them, the connections map.

How to read partner entries.

Each partner or affiliation shows the status the public record gives it. A status of “Unverified” does not say an affiliation is false; it says no source in this repository establishes it. The index describes no role, contract or scope of work for any partner. Datasets are third-party reference data that INSTAR did not produce. See the affiliation register for the evidence behind each status.
